Die Well Media

Helping men of God go all-in on eternity.

Die Well Media exists to help men of God go all-in on eternity. We believe that the enemy is at war for the hearts, time, and talents of men of God, to distract them from eternity, from the Kingdom of God, because the enemy knows this: if he gets the men, he'll get the families; if he gets the families, he'll get the churches; and if he gets the churches, he knows that he'll "win" (even though he ultimately can't win).

Die Well Media came into being as a result of the state of men in mainstream media. There are men who are extremely disciplined with no hope of eternity. They will be disciplined until the day they die, and then they will go to hell. What was all of it for?

Then there are men who are completely passive, men who have detached from the important things of life to live through a console built by men. How sad it is to waste your life away playing games like little boys who never grew up.

The men who die well are the men who live on mission. We are on this earth for a purpose, and in order to die well we ought to make every effort to achieve that purpose. Yes, we talk a lot about discipline, but we believe it is a means to an end, with the end being bringing more glory to Jesus Christ with our disciplined bodies.

To die well is to live on purpose. To live intentionally. To go all-in on eternity. So please, join us on this mission to die well and help all men of God detach from this world and go all-in on eternity.
