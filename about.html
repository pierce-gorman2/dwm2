About

Die Well Media exists to help men of God go all-in on eternity.

Founders Kaydon, Luis, and Pierce grew up on one side of the ditch: all-out discipline for discipline's sake. After the Lord Jesus saved them, they realized that true discipline is a means to an end: to bring glory to Jesus Christ our King. (Click here to learn more about the abstract concept of "bringing glory to Jesus.")

There are a myriad of daily decisions that ought to be subjected to our Lord Jesus in order to die well. The task is daunting and the calling is high, but, men of God, this is what we were created for. We were created for glory. While the majority of the world is entertaining themselves to death, men of God, rise up! Fight the good fight, burn the boats, press on. The gates of hell don't stand a chance!

At Die Well Media, we aim to create premium content for the men of God who are fighting the darkness in this world. We pray that everything we put out helps you press on in this fight, helps you burn the boats, and helps you go all-in on eternity. Your children's children are depending on you. Press on.

Soli Deo Gloria,

Bio

Pierce is a third-generation restaurant franchisee trying to steward his family's legacy wisely. He sees his work as his mission field, showing the love of Christ in a predominantly secular industry. Ever since he got saved at 18, his passion has been to study and teach the Word of God. Die Well Media is an overflow of the passion God instilled in him.

Pierce and his wife Hannah have one newborn son, Ezra Shepherd.

His hobbies include reading, running, and camping/hiking.

Favorite verse: Romans 11:36
