Pierce Gorman

Bio

Pierce is a third-generation restaurant franchisee trying to steward his family's legacy wisely. He sees his work as his mission field, showing the love of Christ in a predominantly secular industry. Ever since he got saved at 18, his passion has been to study and teach the Word of God. Die Well Media is an overflow of the passion God instilled in him.

Pierce and his wife Hannah have one newborn son, Ezra Shepherd.

His hobbies include reading, running, and camping/hiking.

Favorite verse: Romans 11:36
